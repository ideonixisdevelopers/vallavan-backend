Account Deletion Policy

App: Vallavan Sports

Effective Date: 2026

Vallavan Sports respects your privacy and gives users control over their personal data. This Account Deletion Policy explains how users can delete their accounts and what happens to their data after deletion.

1. User Account Deletion

Users have the right to request deletion of their Vallavan Sports account at any time. When an account is deleted, the user will no longer be able to access the application or its services.

2. How to Request Account Deletion

Users can request account deletion using the following methods:

Method 1: Through the Application
  • Go to the Profile or Settings section
  • Select Delete Account
  • Confirm the deletion request
Method 2: Email Request

Users may also request account deletion by sending an email to:

Email: support@vallavansports.com

Please include your registered phone number or email address used in the app.

3. Data Deletion

  • Personal information such as name, email, phone number and profile data will be permanently deleted.
  • Associated training and profile information will also be removed.

4. Data Retention

Certain information may be retained temporarily for legal, security or fraud prevention purposes. Such data will be deleted once it is no longer required.

5. Processing Time

Account deletion requests are typically processed within 7 business days after verification.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about account deletion please contact us:

Email: support@vallavansports.com
